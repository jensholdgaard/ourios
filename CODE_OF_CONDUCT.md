# Code of Conduct

This is about software and code. Ourios is a technical project, and this
document says how we work together on it. It does not take positions on
anything else, and it asks contributors not to bring anything else into
project spaces.

## How we work

1. **Contributions are judged on their merit.** Code, designs, RFCs, and
   reviews stand or fall on technical quality, correctness, and fit with the
   project's stated direction ([`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md), [`docs/rfcs/`](docs/rfcs/)). Nothing about the
   contributor is a factor.
2. **Keep it professional.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Keep discussion
   on the work. Personal attacks, harassment, and deliberate disruption are
   out of bounds. So are unrelated topics: project spaces are for the project.
3. **The maintainers decide.** The maintainers set direction, resolve
   disputes, and may decline or remove any contribution or participant that
   works against the project. Their decisions on project matters are final.
4. **Assume good faith.** Ask before assuming. Give authors and reviewers the
   reading you would want yourself.
5. **Own your work.** Sign off your commits ([DCO](CONTRIBUTING.md#developer-certificate-of-origin-sign-off)). Say what you tested. If you
   broke something, say so and fix it.

## Where this applies

Every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any channel or event where someone is representing Ourios.

## Reporting

If someone is acting against this document, write to the maintainer at
<jens@holdgaard.org>. For anything sensitive, use GitHub's private
[security advisory](https://github.com/jensholdgaard/ourios/security/advisories/new)
form instead. Reports are read by a maintainer, kept in confidence, and
answered. Outcomes range from a conversation to removal from the project, at
the maintainers' judgement and in proportion to the conduct.
